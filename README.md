# My Top 10 TV Series
Here is a list of my all-time favorite shows, from **Mystery Thrillers**, *period romances*, to ~~cliche~~ beautiful heartwarming kdramas.

---
## My Official Ranking
1. **Criminal Minds** 🔍

<sub>   My number one on the list. 18 seasons and I'm still here for more. This series is about an elite team of DBI profilers known as the **Behavioral Analysis Unit (BAU)**. The BAU investigates crimes by analyzing the **psychology, behavior, and patterns** of the perpetrators whom they refer to as **""unsubs""** or the unknown subjects. </sub>

2. **Bridgerton** 📜

<sub> Set in the competitive world of Regency-era London high society, this lush period drama follows the eight close-knit **Bridgerton siblings** as they navigate the marriage mart. Between **scandalous gossip** from the mysterious Lady Whistledown, orchestral pop covers, and **steamy slow-burn romances**, every season brings a new love story to obsess over. </sub>

3. **Stranger Things** 🧇🔦🚲📺

<sub> A love letter to 1980s sci-fi and horror classics set in the small town of Hawkins, Indiana. When a young boy vanishes, his friends, family, and the local police uncover **secret government experiments**, terrifying supernatural forces, and a mysterious girl with **telekinetic powers** named Eleven—all linked to a dark alternate dimension called the **Upside Down**. </sub>

4. **Twenty Five, Twenty One** 🤺📟

<sub> Set against the backdrop of the 1998 South Korean financial crisis, this nostalgic coming-of-age K-drama follows **Na Hee-do**, a passionate teenage fencer whose high school team gets disbanded, and **Baek Yi-jin**, a young man trying to rebuild his life after his family goes bankrupt. It is an unforgettable story about **youth, resilience, friendship, and bittersweet first love**. </sub>

5. **Law & Order: Special Victims Unit** ⚖️

<sub> Television's longest-running primetime live-action drama follows the dedicated detectives of the NYPD's **Special Victims Unit**, led by the iconic **Olivia Benson**. Each episode tackles intense, emotionally complex **sexually based offenses and sensitive crimes**, blending gritty street-level detective work with gripping **courtroom justice**. </sub>
  
6. **Gilmore Girls** ☕🍂🧺🧸

<sub> The ultimate cozy comfort show centered on the fast-talking, coffee-addicted mother-daughter duo **Lorelai and Rory Gilmore** in the quirky small town of Stars Hollow. Packed with **witty pop-culture banter**, seasonal town festivals, family drama, and memorable romances, it feels like a warm hug every autumn. </sub>

7. **Dexter** 🔪 🩸

<sub> A gripping psychological crime thriller about **Dexter Morgan**, a forensic blood-spatter analyst for the Miami Metro Police Department who leads a secret double life. Guided by a strict moral code taught by his adoptive father, Dexter channels his dark urges into hunting down **murderers and criminals who slipped through the cracks** of the justice system. </sub>

8. **Our Beloved Summer** 🍃🎥

<sub> A grounded, heartfelt romantic comedy about **Choi Ung and Kook Yeon-soo**, two high school ex-lovers who promised never to see each other again after a messy breakup. Years later, a **documentary they filmed in high school** unexpectedly goes viral, forcing them back in front of the cameras—and forcing them to confront their **unresolved feelings**. </sub>

9. **9-1-1** 📞🚔🚓🚨

<sub> A high-stakes procedural drama that dives into the adrenaline-fueled lives of Los Angeles first responders—**police officers, paramedics, firefighters, and dispatchers**. From wild, larger-than-life emergencies to deeply personal rescue missions, the show balances **heart-pounding action** with the strong bonds of a tight-knit crew. </sub>

10. **The Walking Dead** 🧟‍♂️

<sub> Based on the acclaimed comic book series, this post-apocalyptic horror drama follows **Rick Grimes** and a ragtag group of survivors fighting to stay alive in a world overrun by flesh-eating **"walkers."** Over time, it becomes less about the zombies and more about **human nature, leadership, and the moral lines** people cross to protect the ones they love. </sub>
